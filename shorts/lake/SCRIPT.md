# The Lake That Went Down the Drain

Original 55-70 s cartoon 3D short. Story mode, one steady narrator, no captions.

## Facts (with sources)
| # | Fact used in the script | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 Nov 1980 (a Thursday), Lake Peigneur, Louisiana: a Texaco-contracted rig drilling for oil pierced the Diamond Crystal salt mine under the lake. | Wikipedia "Lake Peigneur"; 64 Parishes "Lake Peigneur Drilling Accident" |
| 2 | Lake was a shallow (~10-11 ft / 3 m) freshwater lake with an island. | Wikipedia; 64 Parishes |
| 3 | Drill bit stuck at ~1,230 ft (1,228 ft); crew heard loud popping, rig tilted; crew of seven fled shortly before the rig collapsed into the lake. | Amusing Planet; Wikipedia |
| 4 | Fresh water dissolved the salt and enlarged the hole over several hours; the lake drained into the mine. | Wikipedia; Amusing Planet |
| 5 | Whirlpool swallowed the rig, 11 barges, a tugboat, trees and ~65 acres of land (incl. part of a garden/island). | Wikipedia; Damn Interesting |
| 6 | A fisherman in a small boat on the lake made it to shore. | Wikipedia (via search summary) |
| 7 | All 55 miners underground escaped; electrician Junius Gaddison saw water rushing in and raised the alarm; they left via the only elevator. No deaths. | Wikipedia; KATC; Amusing Planet |
| 8 | The Delcambre Canal flowed backwards into the lake, making for a few days the tallest waterfall in Louisiana (Wikipedia: 164 ft; other sources ~150 ft, so the script says no number). | Wikipedia; 64 Parishes |
| 9 | The lake refilled in about two days; nine of the 11 barges popped back up after the refill. | Wikipedia; Damn Interesting; geoprac.net |
| 10 | The lake changed from fresh water to salt water. | Wikipedia |
| 11 | Federal report (MSHA, Aug 1981) documented it but named no official cause. | Wikipedia |

Not used (sources disagree): final lake depth (200 ft vs 1,300 ft), exact waterfall height, settlement amounts.

Analogy: pulling the plug in a bathtub, except the drain is made of salt and water dissolves salt, so the drain grows.

## Script (target ~3.7 words/s)
| Beat | Line |
|---|---|
| Hook | In 1980, an oil crew drilled one small hole and accidentally pulled the plug on an entire lake. |
| Setting | Lake Peigneur, Louisiana. Ten feet deep, calm, full of fish, with a fancy garden on the shore. |
| Action 1 | A Texaco rig is drilling for oil right in the middle of it. Twelve hundred feet down, the drill gets stuck. |
| Action 2 | Then the whole rig starts to tilt. The crew hears loud pops, jumps in a boat, and gets away just in time. |
| Action 3 | Behind them, the lake starts to spin. A whirlpool swallows the rig. Then a tugboat. Then eleven barges, one after another. |
| Action 4 | A fisherman floors his tiny boat and makes it to shore. Even the canal to the sea turns around and pours in backwards, as a waterfall. |
| Mystery | Wait. Where is all that water going? |
| Snap | Right under the lake was a salt mine, with fifty-five miners inside. An electrician spots water rushing down a tunnel and sounds the alarm. |
| Double line | Luckily, every single miner gets out. Unluckily, the lake doesn't. |
| Explanation | You see, the drill had punched straight through the roof of the mine. Picture a bathtub. Pull the plug, and the water swirls down the drain. But this drain was made of salt. And water dissolves salt. So the faster the lake drained, the bigger the drain got. |
| Consequence | In a few hours, the lake was gone. It took two days for the canal to fill it back up. |
| Twist | And then, one by one, nine of the barges popped back up like corks. The lake came back too. Just saltier. |

## Picture notes (for the shot plan)
- World: shallow lake with a small island, oak trees and a garden on the shore (Kenney nature kit), a lattice drilling rig, tugboat, barge string, a fisherman's skiff, a canal with a wooden lock/bank at one end.
- Underground cutaway for the mine: salt-white tunnels, miners with hard hats, a single elevator cage.
- Light: day for the hook and setting; the whirlpool runs under a darkening, stormy grade; golden for the refill and the twist.
- Effect cards: spray, ripples, "pop" sparkles on the drill, the waterfall, cork bobbing barges.
