## The story (verify every fact against sources before writing the script)
- April 1970: an oxygen tank explodes on Apollo 13 on the way to the Moon.
- The three astronauts shelter in the lunar module (Aquarius), built for 2 people for ~2 days; they need it for 3 people for ~4 days.
- Carbon dioxide builds up in the cabin. The command module's spare lithium hydroxide canisters are SQUARE; the lunar module's sockets are ROUND.
- In Houston, engineers put on a table only what the crew had on board (plastic bags, cardboard from flight-plan covers, hoses, a sock, duct tape) and invent an adapter; they read the build steps to the crew over the radio. It works and the CO2 drops.
